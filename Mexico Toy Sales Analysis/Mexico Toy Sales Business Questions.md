# Mexico Toy Sales — Business Questions & Analytical Objectives

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to use sales, product, store, inventory, and time-based data to answer practical business questions and support better decision-making through Microsoft Fabric and Power BI.

The final report is organized into five analytical areas:

- Executive Overview
- Product Analysis
- Store Analysis
- Inventory Analysis
- Time Intelligence

## 2. Executive / Management Questions

1. What are the total sales, total profit, total units sold, and overall profit margin?
2. How are sales and profit trending over time?
3. Which products are the top contributors to overall sales?
4. Which stores are the top contributors to overall sales?
5. What are the major product and store-level insights management should know?
6. What actions can be taken to improve sales, profitability, and inventory decisions?

## 3. Product Analysis Questions

### Sales Performance

1. Which products sell the highest number of units?
2. Which products generate the highest sales revenue?
3. Which products generate the highest total profit?
4. Which products contribute strongly to sales but relatively less to profit?
5. Which products have the highest profit margins?
6. Which products are among the lowest performers in sales or profitability?

### Product Cost and Pricing

7. Which products have the highest product cost?
8. How does product price relate to total profit?
9. Which products combine high sales with high profitability?
10. Which products have high sales but comparatively low profit margins?

## 4. Store Analysis Questions

### Sales and Profitability

1. Which stores generate the highest sales?
2. Which stores generate the highest profit?
3. Which cities generate the highest total sales?
4. What is the average sales per store within each city?
5. Which stores have the highest profit margins?
6. Which stores contribute the most to total profit?
7. Are there stores with strong sales but relatively weaker profitability?

### Inventory and Store Performance

8. Which stores hold the highest inventory levels?
9. How is inventory distributed across stores?
10. Which stores may require more supply based on inventory and sales performance?

## 5. Inventory Analysis Questions

### Inventory Position

1. What is the current total inventory?
2. What is the total value of the current inventory?
3. Which product categories hold the largest inventory quantities?
4. Which products have the highest inventory levels?
5. Which products have the lowest inventory levels?
6. What is the average inventory per store?
7. What is the average inventory per product?

### Inventory Monitoring

8. Which products appear relatively low in stock compared with other products?
9. Which products appear relatively high in stock compared with other products?
10. Which products and stores deserve further investigation for inventory balancing?

> **Important analytical note:** In this project, the low/high inventory product visuals are based on Top N ranking (the lowest and highest inventory products). They should not automatically be interpreted as formal "low stock" or "overstocked" conditions unless the business provides explicit inventory thresholds.

## 6. Time Intelligence Questions

1. What were the sales and profit results over the most recent 30-day period?
2. What were the sales and profit results over the most recent three months?
3. What are the current year-to-date sales and profit figures?
4. How do current-year sales compare with last year?
5. How do current-year profit compare with last year?
6. What are the sales trends across the available years?
7. What are the profit trends across the available years?
8. Are sales and profitability improving or declining over time?

## 7. Key Business Insights the Report Can Surface

The report is designed to identify patterns such as:

- A product may have very high sales but relatively low profit because of its cost structure.
- A product with lower sales volume may still be highly attractive because of a strong profit margin.
- The highest-selling store is not necessarily the store with the highest profit margin.
- Average sales per store can reveal differences between cities that total city sales alone may hide.
- Inventory concentration can highlight products or categories that hold a large amount of stock.
- Recent-period and year-over-year metrics can show whether business performance is improving or weakening.

## 8. Example Business Recommendations

Based on the findings from the report, management could consider:

- Increasing marketing focus on products with strong profitability and growth potential.
- Reviewing pricing and cost structures for products with high sales but relatively low profit.
- Increasing supply to stores that combine strong demand with comparatively lower inventory.
- Investigating products with very high inventory relative to their sales performance.
- Monitoring year-over-year sales and profit trends to identify areas requiring corrective action.

## 9. Business Value

The purpose of these questions is not only to describe what happened, but to help the business understand **where performance is strongest, where profitability is weaker, where inventory is concentrated, and where action may be needed**.
